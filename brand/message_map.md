# Lumora Message Map

## Audience 1: Creators (16+) — Primary

**Core message:** Learn AI tools by building real projects. Ship work. Open opportunities.

**Key points:**
- Hands-on from minute one — no passive video lectures
- Build portfolio-grade projects with tools you already use (CapCut, Make.com, Canva)
- Skills that translate to freelance gigs, content careers, and creative side income
- Guided workflows, not just theory

**Proof points:**
- 45 lessons across 4 high-demand domains (video, automation, content writing, music)
- Every lesson produces a tangible output
- Platform-specific training (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube — not generic)

## Audience 2: Parents

**Core message:** Lumora teaches real-world AI skills through guided, age-appropriate creative projects.

**Key points:**
- Structured learning path with clear outcomes
- Portfolio outputs they can show (not just screen time)
- Skills that matter for college apps, internships, and future careers
- Safe, curated tools — no open-ended AI chat

**Proof points:**
- Designed for ages 16+
- Every lesson has defined deliverables
- AI tutor provides guided help, not open-ended conversation

## Audience 3: Schools & Educators

**Core message:** A ready-made AI curriculum that produces student portfolios, not just test scores.

**Key points:**
- Curriculum-aligned creative AI training
- Students build real outputs they can showcase
- Covers high-demand skills: video editing, automation, content creation
- Progress tracking and structured learning paths

**Proof points:**
- 45 lessons with clear objectives and time estimates
- School licensing available
- Designed to complement existing programs, not replace them
