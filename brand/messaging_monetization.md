# Lumora Monetization Messaging Guardrails

## Framing Rule

Monetization is a natural outcome of building real skills — never the primary hook.

Present earning as **"paid opportunities over time"**, not **"get rich quick."**

Monetization lessons still exist in the curriculum. This guide governs how we talk about them in marketing, UI copy, and lesson descriptions.

## Allowed Phrases

- "Turn skills into paid opportunities over time"
- "Build freelance-ready skills"
- "Open new career paths"
- "Create work that earns"
- "Build a portfolio that opens doors"
- "Skills that translate to real income"
- "Freelance-curious? Start here."

## Avoided Phrases

- "Monetize fast"
- "Earn in 30 days or less"
- "Make money with AI"
- "Get rich" / "passive income guaranteed"
- "Start your empire"
- "The only platform that..."
- "Unlock the secrets to..."
- "Side hustle" (as primary framing)

## Lesson Copy Examples

### Before (old style)
> "Monetization Strategies: Learn to monetize your video skills in 30 days or less. Turn AI into a money-making machine."

### After (new style)
> "Monetization Strategies: Explore how to turn your video skills into freelance offers, brand partnerships, and paid content — with real examples and templates."

## Landing Page Rules

1. Hero sections lead with skill/output, not income
2. Monetization can appear in feature lists but not as the #1 bullet
3. Avoid specific income claims or timelines
4. Use "opportunities" framing, not "income" framing
