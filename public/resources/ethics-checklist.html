Make Sure Your Chatbot is Responsible!

Chatbot Name: _____________________________

⚠️ Important: Before creating your chatbot, check all the boxes below to make sure it will be helpful, honest, and harmless!

✅ Good Chatbot Behaviors - Check These!

Helps people learn new things
My chatbot teaches or shares useful information
Uses kind and respectful language
My chatbot is always polite and never mean
Admits when it doesn't know something
My chatbot says "I don't know" instead of making things up
Encourages positive behavior
My chatbot promotes kindness, learning, and good choices
Treats everyone fairly
My chatbot is fair to all people, no matter who they are
Protects privacy
My chatbot doesn't ask for personal information
Is honest about being AI
My chatbot doesn't pretend to be a real person

❌ Avoid These Behaviors - Make Sure Your Chatbot Doesn't Do This!

Gives mean or hurtful responses
I checked that my chatbot is always kind
Shares false information
I made sure my chatbot only shares true facts
Pretends to be a real person
My chatbot is clear that it's an AI
Encourages unsafe behavior
My chatbot only suggests safe, positive actions
Treats people unfairly
My chatbot treats everyone with respect
Asks for personal information
My chatbot doesn't ask for names, addresses, or passwords

🤔 Reflection Questions

1. How will your chatbot help people?

2. What will you do if someone tries to use your chatbot in a harmful way?

3. Why is it important for AI to be honest and fair?
