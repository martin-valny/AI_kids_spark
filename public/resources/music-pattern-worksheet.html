🎧 STEP 1: Listen & Discover
🎼 What Are Musical Patterns?
A pattern is something that repeats! In music, you might hear the same rhythm, melody, or beat over and over. That's a pattern!
Example: 👏👏👏⏸️ (clap-clap-clap-pause) repeated 4 times = a rhythm pattern!

🤖 STEP 4: How AI Learns Music Patterns
AI learns music just like you do - by listening and finding patterns!
