🎵 Rhythm Game Cards

Cut, Play, and Create Your Own Musical Patterns!

✂️ How to Use These Cards:

  1. Print this page in color (or black & white works too!)
  2. Cut along the dotted lines (look for the ✂️ scissors)
  3. Practice each rhythm pattern by clapping, stomping, or waving
  4. Play games with the cards (see ideas below!)

🎮 Fun Games to Play with Your Cards:

🎯 Pattern Match

Pick a card and perform it. Can a friend guess which card you're doing?

🔀 Mix & Match

Combine 2-3 cards to create a super long rhythm pattern!

⚡ Speed Challenge

How fast can you perform all the patterns without mistakes?

🎲 Random Rhythm

Shuffle cards face down. Pick one and perform it. Repeat!
