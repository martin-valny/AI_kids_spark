📝 The Video Prompt Formula

🎯 Perfect Prompt = Subject + Action + Setting + Details + Style

Example:

"A friendly robot [subject] waving hello [action] in a colorful garden [setting] with flowers and butterflies [details], cartoon style [style]"

❌ vs ✅ Prompt Examples

❌ TOO SIMPLE

"a cat"

Problem: Not enough details!

✅ MUCH BETTER

"a fluffy orange cat playing with a ball of yarn in a cozy living room, sunlight streaming through window, realistic style"

Why: Specific details create better videos!

❌ TOO VAGUE

"space"

Problem: What about space?

✅ MUCH BETTER

"an astronaut floating near a space station with Earth visible below, stars twinkling in background, cinematic style"

Why: Clear scene and atmosphere!

💡 Pro Tips for Great Video Prompts

1. Be Specific About Movement

Instead of: "a bird"

Try: "a blue bird flying through clouds" or "a bird landing on a branch"

2. Describe the Atmosphere

Add words like: sunny, rainy, foggy, bright, dark, peaceful, exciting

Example: "a peaceful forest at sunrise with morning mist"

3. Include Camera Angles (Optional)

Try: "close-up shot", "wide angle view", "bird's eye view"

Example: "close-up shot of a flower blooming"

4. Keep It Short and Clear

Aim for 1-2 sentences per scene

Too long = confusing. Too short = not enough detail.
