🎨 AI Art Style Reference Guide

Learn different art styles to use in your prompts!

🖼️ Realistic Photo

Looks like a real photograph taken with a camera
Keywords: realistic photo, photorealistic, high detail, professional photography

🎨 Cartoon Style

Fun, colorful, simplified shapes like in cartoons
Keywords: cartoon style, animated, colorful, simple shapes

🌸 Watercolor Painting

Soft, flowing colors that blend together
Keywords: watercolor painting, soft colors, flowing, artistic

💻 Digital Art

Modern, clean, created on computer
Keywords: digital art, modern, clean lines, vibrant

🎮 Pixel Art

Made of tiny squares like old video games
Keywords: pixel art, 8-bit, retro game style, blocky

🖌️ Oil Painting

Rich, textured, like classical paintings
Keywords: oil painting, classical art, textured, rich colors

✨ Anime Style

Japanese animation style with big eyes
Keywords: anime style, manga, Japanese animation

✏️ Sketch Drawing

Pencil or pen drawing, black and white
Keywords: sketch drawing, pencil art, line art, black and white

🎭 3D Render

Computer-generated 3D objects
Keywords: 3D render, CGI, computer graphics, smooth

🌟 Fantasy Art

Magical, dreamy, otherworldly
Keywords: fantasy art, magical, ethereal, dreamy

📰 Comic Book

Bold lines, action-packed, speech bubbles
Keywords: comic book style, bold lines, action, graphic novel

🎪 Pop Art

Bright colors, bold patterns, like Andy Warhol
Keywords: pop art, bright colors, bold, retro

💡 How to Use This Guide:

  1. Choose a style that matches your idea
  2. Add the keywords to the END of your prompt
  3. Example: "A cat in space, realistic photo style"
  4. Try the same subject in different styles!
